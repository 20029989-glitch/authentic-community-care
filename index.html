About us

Authentic Community Care Pty Ltd is a disability support provider based in Adamstown, Newcastle NSW. We believe everyone has the right to make their own choices and live the life they want, and our job is to support that.

We work with NDIS participants and their families, and we’re happy to talk through how your plan is managed and which of our supports fit.

What you can expect

  • Person-centred support built around your goals
  • Respect for your choices, culture and privacy
  • Compliance with the NDIS Code of Conduct and Practice Standards
  • NDIS Worker Screening for our workers where required
  • Clear service agreements and a simple way to raise concerns

Referrals and funding

Anyone can refer: you, your family, a support coordinator, plan manager, Local Area Coordinator, GP or hospital discharge team. Self-referral is welcome.

We’ll ask for your name and contact details, the supports you’re looking for, how your plan is managed, your suburb and when you’d like to start.

We charge in line with the NDIS Pricing Arrangements and Price Limits, and your service agreement shows every cost before you sign.

For support coordinators

  • Clear, prompt replies to referrals
  • Honest advice on current availability
  • Regular updates on how supports are going
  • Accurate invoices that match your participant’s plan

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most. If yours isn’t here, just ask.

Do I need to be an NDIS participant?

Our supports are funded through NDIS plans. If you’re not a participant yet, we can point you to the NDIS or your Local Area Coordinator to help you apply.

Can I choose my support workers?

Yes. Tell us what matters to you, such as gender, interests, language or personality, and we’ll do our best to match you. If it isn’t working, you can ask for a change.

How is my plan managed, and does it matter?

Plans can be managed by you, by a plan manager, or by the NDIA. This affects how we invoice, so tell us when you enquire and we’ll explain what applies to you.

What is a service agreement?

It’s a written agreement that sets out the supports we’ll deliver, when, how and at what cost, plus your rights and our cancellation terms. We explain it in plain language and you can take time to read it.

Can my family be involved?

Yes, if you want them to be. You decide who we talk to and what we share.

Can I change provider later?

Yes. It’s your plan and your choice, and you can change providers at any time.

What if I’m not happy with something?

Tell us and we’ll work with you to put it right. You can also contact the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission on 1800 035 544. Raising a concern will never affect the support you receive.

Where do you work?

We’re based in Adamstown and support people across Newcastle and the Hunter. Ask us about your suburb.
